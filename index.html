Our story

Keep Rollin' started with a simple obsession: the perfect roll. A golden, flaky paratha holding juicy charcoal-grilled boti, creamy sauce and a kick of fresh chutney in every bite.

From our grill in Shah Faisal Colony, we marinate chicken and beef in our own spice blends and cook it over real charcoal. Fresh ingredients, a clean kitchen and quick service, because hunger doesn't wait.
